# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

## Costs
Standard shipping is free for orders over $100. Below that it costs $9.95.
Express shipping costs $19.95 for parcels up to 5 kg and $29.95 for parcels up to 20 kg.
Parcels heavier than 20 kg ship as freight and need a custom quote.

## Lost parcels
If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, open a high priority ticket.
We reship or refund lost parcels at no cost to the customer.

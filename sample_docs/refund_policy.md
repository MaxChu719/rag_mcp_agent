# Refund Policy

## Standard refunds
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of delivery for any unopened item.
Opened items can be refunded within 14 days of delivery, minus a 15% restocking fee.

## Electronics
Opened electronics (headphones, speakers, chargers) are only refundable if faulty.
Faulty electronics are covered by a 12-month warranty: we repair, replace or refund.

## How refunds are processed
Every refund request needs a support ticket. Refunds go back to the original payment
method within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse.

# Product FAQ

## Aurora Headphones
Battery life is up to 40 hours with noise cancelling off and 30 hours with it on.
A full charge takes 2 hours over USB-C. The headphones weigh 250 g.

## Nimbus Speaker
The Nimbus is water resistant (IPX7) and can be submerged in 1 m of water for 30 minutes.
Two Nimbus speakers can be paired for stereo sound from the companion app.
